Our Story

A Quarter Century of Pharmaceutical Excellence

Founded in 1998 in Mumbai, India, NexaGen Pharmaceuticals has grown from a domestic formulations company into a globally respected, vertically integrated pharmaceutical manufacturer. Our facilities are inspected and approved by WHO, US FDA, EU EMA, and multiple stringent regulatory authorities.

We combine the agility of a specialized manufacturer with the scale and compliance capabilities of a multi-national — enabling our clients to access high-quality medicines at competitive cost without compromising on patient safety.

Patient Safety First

Every decision is filtered through a singular lens: will this protect the patient? No compromise on quality or compliance.

Scientific Rigor

Our 400-member R&D team pursues evidence-based innovation, from molecule optimization to delivery system engineering.

Global Accessibility

We believe life-saving medicines should reach every patient, irrespective of geography. We supply to 60+ countries across 5 continents.

Sustainable Ops

ISO 14001-certified environmental management. We've reduced carbon intensity by 38% since 2018 and target carbon neutrality by 2032.

Quality Milestones

2002

First WHO-GMP Certification

Achieved WHO-GMP certification for our Andheri solid dosage facility, opening access to UN procurement agencies and generic donor markets.

2009

EU GMP Approval — Sterile Block

Greenfield sterile manufacturing block passed EU MHRA inspection with zero critical observations, enabling direct export to EU member states.

2015

US FDA Registration

Three manufacturing units registered under US FDA 21 CFR Part 210/211 following a successful pre-approval inspection (PAI) with no Form 483 observations.

2022

ICH Q13 Continuous Manufacturing

Commissioned India's first ICH Q13-compliant continuous tablet manufacturing line, reducing batch release time by 65% and improving process analytical technology (PAT) integration.

Quality Systems

Electronic Batch Records (EBR)

Fully paperless batch documentation with real-time deviation detection and 21 CFR Part 11-compliant audit trails across all production lines.

Validated LIMS Integration

Laboratory Information Management System (LIMS) integrated with QC instruments for automated results capture, OOS investigation, and CoA generation.

Cold Chain & Distribution QMS

GDP-compliant temperature-controlled distribution network with IoT-enabled real-time cold chain monitoring from dispatch to last-mile delivery.

Pharmacovigilance & Signal Detection

Dedicated PV team with 24/7 adverse event intake, signal detection using WHO VigiBase integration, and PSUR submission compliance in all registered markets.

Solid Dosage

Andheri Facility, Mumbai

Our flagship 280,000 sq ft solid dosage campus houses 8 granulation suites, 14 tablet compression lines, coating equipment, and blister/strip packaging lines. Capacity: 500M solid units/year.

Sterile Manufacturing

Turbhe Sterile Block, Navi Mumbai

Class A/B aseptic filling facility with 4 filling lines for ampoules, vials, and prefilled syringes, plus lyophilization capability (12 lyophilizers). Capacity: 80M units/year.

OTC & Consumer

Himachal Pradesh Consumer Campus

HP Tax Incentive Zone facility dedicated to OTC and nutraceutical production. Softgel, liquid, semi-solid, and aerosol lines. Capacity: 1.2B units/year. ISO 22000 certified.

Research & Development

Our 400-scientist R&D organization operates from three innovation centers, with deep expertise in formulation development, analytical method development, and regulatory science.

Advanced Formulation Development

Expertise in NDDS, nanotechnology-based drug delivery, OROS, lipid-based drug delivery, hot-melt extrusion, and spray drying for poorly soluble APIs.

Analytical & Stability Sciences

Regulatory Affairs & IP Strategy
